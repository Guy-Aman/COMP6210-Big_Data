Column Descriptors

player_name - Full name of the football player.

nationality - Player's national team or country. Some raw records may contain
"Unknown" and should be cleaned or inferred where possible.

club - The player's club at the time of the recorded match.

position - The player's primary playing position, such as GK, CB, CM, CAM, LW,
RW, ST, Forward, Midfielder, Defender, or Goalkeeper.

age - Player's age at the time of the season or competition record.

market_value_eur_m - Estimated player market value in millions of euros. Missing
values may appear in the raw dataset and should be handled during data curation.

season - The season associated with the match record.

competition - The competition in which the match was played. Expected cleaned
values include FIFA World Cup 2026, League, Champions League, Domestic Cup, and
World Cup Qualifiers.

match_id - Unique identifier for a match record.

minutes_played - Number of minutes played by the player in the match. Values
greater than 90 may occur because of stoppage time or extra time.

goals - Number of goals scored by the player in the match.

assists - Number of assists made by the player in the match.

shots_on_target - Number of shots on target made by the player in the match.

pass_accuracy_pct - Player's pass accuracy percentage in the match.

tackles - Number of tackles made by the player in the match.

distance_covered_km - Total distance covered by the player in the match,
measured in kilometres.

match_rating - Player's overall match rating.

yellow_card - Binary indicator showing whether the player received a yellow
card in the match. A value of 1 indicates yes, and 0 indicates no.

red_card - Binary indicator showing whether the player received a red card in
the match. A value of 1 indicates yes, and 0 indicates no.

fouls_committed - Number of fouls committed by the player in the match.

injured_flag - Binary indicator showing whether the player was marked as
injured in the match record. A value of 1 indicates yes, and 0 indicates no.
